# Remote Sensing Fall 2026 Final Project

## Abstract
Project repo for remote sensing fall 2026 class.

The melt rate with respect to marine terminating glaciers in the Arctic, in particular Greenland, are of large interest when it comes to better understanding the overall dramatic change happening in the Arctic and understanding global sea rise levels. My project goal is to compare Disko Bay across different years to compare low vs high melt years and to be able to connect this data with my own modeling domain and outputs for comparison. The years I aim to use are (2018, 2019, 2020, 2021). I will focus on analyzing the differencees betweeen the satellite data from MODIS SST and Sea Ice Concentration from NSDIC and compare these to my personal model outputs, furthering the work on my own thesis. 

## Datasets
I aim to use the following datasets:
- MODIS / Aqua Terra SST 
- SMAP SSS (stretch goal)
- Sea Ice Concentration from NSIDC
